Add a garment

Copy the chart off your product page. The size names are yours — XS…XXL, 38/40/42, Small — and the numbers are the finished garment's, in whichever unit you already print. The order matters only for a tie: type them smallest first.

Columns

One column for each thing your chart prints. The first column this app can measure decides the size — chest or bust for a top, waist for trousers — and the other measured columns are checked against the shopper too. A column it cannot read off a body, such as a body length, is shown to the shopper beside their size and never changes the answer.

Your size charts

Each one has a link of its own. Follow it to check it yourself: it is exactly what a customer sees.

A chart is the garment's numbers, never anybody's. A shopper's clip is measured on their own phone, nothing from it is stored, and nothing from it reaches you — the only thing that travels is the chart you typed, going the other way.

How it reaches a shopper

On your product page

Paste the popup code

Every chart card has a snippet. Drop it once onto the product page and a Find my size button appears there. Pressing it opens the size finder in a popup over your page — no new tab, no basket left behind.

Anywhere else

Or just use the link

The link on its own works too: put it in a product description, an Instagram bio, an order-confirmation email. It opens the same size finder, and it still knows which garment it is for.

What the shopper does

One slow turn, one size

They record a 360° video on their phone, say who it is of, and are shown their measurements, their size in your chart, and the numbers that size was read from.
