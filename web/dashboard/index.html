Add a garment

Copy the chart off your product page. The size names are yours — XS…XXL, 38/40/42, Small — and the numbers are the finished garment's, in whichever unit you already print. The order matters only for a tie: type them smallest first.

Each column represents one measurement; the first valid one determines the size, while any remaining measurements are checked for fit or displayed as extra information without altering the size.

Your size charts

Each one has a link of its own. Follow it to check it yourself: it is exactly what a customer sees.

A chart is the garment's numbers, never anybody's. A shopper's clip is measured on their own phone, nothing from it is stored, and nothing from it reaches you — the only thing that travels is the chart you typed, going the other way.

How to add "Find My Size" to your site

One line of HTML per garment. No install, no build step, no account for your customers.

Step 1 — Copy the code

Each garment card above has a box labelled Popup code for your product page. Click inside it, then Copy (or press Ctrl/Cmd+C). It looks like this: 

<script src="https://damii-lola.github.io/3dGarments/embed.js" data-chart="YOUR_CODE" data-label="Find My Size"></script>

That single line is the entire integration. The data-chart part tells it which size chart to use; everything else is optional.

Step 2 — Paste it at the top of your product page

Open your product page's HTML (or your platform's "Custom HTML", "Additional scripts", or "Code injection" box) and paste that line at the very top of your <body>, before anything else. The button will float bottom-right as a FAB.

  • Shopify: Online Store → Themes → Edit code → product page template → paste at the top of <body>.
  • WordPress / WooCommerce: Use a "Custom HTML" block or "Code injection" box on the product page.
  • Squarespace: Use a Code block set to "Embed" and paste the line.
  • Any other site: Paste the <script> tag right after the opening <body> tag.

Paste it once per garment. A page selling three garments gets three floating buttons, one per size chart.

Step 3 — Test it first

Before publishing, click Preview the popup on the garment card. A real popup opens over the dashboard running the exact same code your customers will get. If it looks right here, it'll look right on your site.

Step 4 — Customise (all optional)

Open the chart card’s Preview & Customise Popup panel to style the floating button and every screen of the size finder to match your brand. Then copy the updated snippet below.

  • Button text: data-label="Find my fit"
  • Colour: data-color="#2563eb" (any CSS colour)

Example: a green button that says "Find My Size": 

<script src="https://damii-lola.github.io/3dGarments/embed.js" data-chart="YOUR_CODE" data-label="Find My Size" data-color="#16a34a"></script>

Or just use the link

The The link field on each card works on its own — drop it into a product description, an Instagram bio, or an order-confirmation email. It opens the same size finder and still knows which garment it is for. No button, no code.

What the shopper does

They click the button, a popup opens over your page (no new tab, basket untouched), they record one slow 360° turn on their phone, and they are shown their measurements and which size of your garment to buy. Nothing is saved — their video never leaves their phone.

If the button doesn't appear

  • Paste as HTML / code, not into a visual text editor that strips <script> tags.
  • Shopify / WordPress / Squarespace: use their "Custom HTML", "Additional scripts", or "Code injection" box.
  • If your site has a strict Content-Security-Policy, allow https://damii-lola.github.io in script-src and frame-src.
