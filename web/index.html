The problem

Returns are quietly killing your margins

Most clothing brands lose thousands every month to size-related returns.

Customers don’t know their real measurements.

Size charts vary wildly between brands.

And “order two sizes and return one” has become normal.

Every return costs you

  • Double shipping
  • Restocking time
  • Damaged or unsellable product
  • Lost profit

The result? Lower margins, higher costs, and frustrated customers.

The solution

A better way to get the size right

Our tool lets shoppers on your product page record a short 360° video of themselves. It reads their body measurements from that video, then recommends the size for that exact product — in seconds.

They see the measurements

Every number is shown, with the reading behind it, so nothing has to be taken on trust.

They get a clear size

Their measurements are scored row by row against the chart you typed for that garment: the worst disagreement decides, not the best one.

Then everything is deleted

The clip is deleted the moment it has been measured. Nothing is kept — not the video, not the frames, not the numbers.

No profiles. No saved measurements. No risk.
