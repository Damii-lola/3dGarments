Find your size

Record a 360° video of yourself and we'll measure you from it. Keep your whole body in frame and hold your arms a hand's width away from your sides — that gap is what lets us tell your torso from your arms. Then stand still and turn all the way around, max 30 seconds.

Trim your clip

Keep the part where you turn all the way around, with your arms held a hand's width from your sides. Drag the sliders to trim off the start and the end.

Make it exact — mark something of a known size

Hold something you know the size of in one frame — a bank card flat against your chest, an A4 sheet, a ruler — then find that frame below and drag the two dots onto its ends. The app then measures you against that instead of against your head, which is the biggest single source of error left.
