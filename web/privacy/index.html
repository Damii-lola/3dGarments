Privacy

Last updated November 2026.

FindMySize measures a shopper’s body from a short video and recommends a size. This page says what is kept and what is not.

What we do not keep

  • The video. It is recorded on the shopper’s phone and never uploaded. Only a few still frames travel to the server, and those frames are deleted as soon as the measurement is finished.
  • The measurements. They are returned to the shopper and not stored on our servers.
  • Any profile of the shopper. There is no shopper account.

What a brand keeps

A brand that uses the dashboard creates an account (email and password) and types size charts. Those charts and the account details are stored so the brand can edit them later. We do not sell them.

What the server sees

For a scan, the server receives still frames, computes measurements, returns a size, and discards the frames. Server logs keep timing and error codes only — no body numbers, no images.

Cookies

We use the minimum needed to keep a brand logged in. No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers.
