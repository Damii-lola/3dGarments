# Human assets: CC0

Everything in this folder is generated by `tools/human/build.py` from:

- **MakeHuman** base mesh `hm08`, macro targets, eye proxy and eye texture:
  https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman. The assets were released as CC0 in 2020.
- **MPFB** `game_engine` rig, skin weights and skin region masks:
  https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2 (see `LICENSE.ASSETS.md`: CC0 1.0).

`detail.png` (eyebrows, scalp, beard) and `underwear.png` are painted procedurally by
`tools/human/paint.py`, and are CC0 too.

CC0 means there are no restrictions, including commercial use of rendered images.
