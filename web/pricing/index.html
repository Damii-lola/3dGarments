Questions

What brands ask first

Short, plain answers about privacy, setup and what a store actually gets.

Book a Demo
Do you store my customers’ videos or measurements?

No. The clip is sent to us, read once, and deleted the moment it has been measured — and nothing is kept afterwards: no video, no frames, no measurements, no history. There is no body profile to leak, because there is no bit of one anywhere.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They tap a button and use the camera they already have. The whole exchange is one slow turn on the spot, and about ten seconds of waiting while the numbers are read.

Is it a plugin for my store?

Not a packaged one yet. You get a snippet to paste onto a product page, which opens the size finder in a popup over that page — your theme is not touched, and the shopper is never sent to a new tab. Packaged plugins for the large platforms are the next piece of work.

How am I charged?

$150 per store per month, for unlimited clothing size charts. That’s just 2.5% to 15% of the total money shop owners lose on size returns per month. There is no checkout on this site yet — the subscription is the next thing we build — so today a store is set up with us on a call.

What does it need?

A phone with a camera, and a video of the customer turning all the way around — thirty seconds is the limit, ten is a good clip. On a desktop, a video already on the device can be uploaded instead.

How accurate is it?

It is an estimate, and it says so, in those words, on the result. Every measurement is shown with the reading behind it, and the size is scored against the chart row by row: the worst disagreement decides, so a marginal call lands on the smaller size rather than a hopeful larger one.

Can I try it before talking to anyone?

Yes, and please do. The scanner on this site is the same one your customers would use.
